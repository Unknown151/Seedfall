Seedfall

A single seed pod is about to fall onto an unnamed world. One colonist, a vault of sleeping embryos, and a lot of time.

The world grows while it's on screen, and keeps going for up to 8 hours while you're away. Pick a folder and Seedfall will keep save.json, a readable chronicle.md and stats.csv in it.

How to watch a world

Seedfall runs itself. Leave it on a screen and check in now and then. The camera wanders on its own; drag or scroll to look around and it drifts back after a while.

CChronicle, towns, people, lore 1–5Nudges: rain, supply pod, inspire, starfall, bloom. Then click the world. Each costs ✨ Reverence, which gathers while the world is on screen. 🕯️Prayers: now and then someone asks the Watcher for something. Answer from the card; grateful people give back more Reverence than it cost. Ignoring them is fine too. ⛵Fishing boats, ferries, harbours with ships trading along the coast, and later planes flying between airfields. Some of it takes a few eras to show up. 🪨Towns build from what their land gives: timber by the woods, stone under the crags, brick by the river. C › Towns shows their stores and what they are known for. 6Speak to your people. Day and night, weather and shadows are set under Sky in the chronicle panel. The world pauses whenever this tab isn't visible (minimised, other tab, PC locked). When you come back it catches up on the time you were gone, up to 8 hours, and tells you what you missed. It picks up where it left off. Festivals, new eras, shrines and answered prayers all add to Reverence.

Speak to your people

They will hear it on the wind. They will argue about it for centuries. They will probably misunderstand it.

The Watcher’s voice

With an Anthropic API key, Claude reads what you say to the colonists and works out how they understand it, argue about it and turn it into customs. It can also write the occasional bit of town gossip about your named people. Without a key your words are still heard, just not understood.

The key is kept in this browser only, never in save.json or any other file. Get one at console.anthropic.com; a separate key with a low monthly spend limit is a good idea. Each call costs a fraction of a cent with Haiku.
